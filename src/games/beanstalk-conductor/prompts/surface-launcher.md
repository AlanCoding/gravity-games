# Surface Launcher Prompt Notes

Need a surface space launcher on Civic Prime. It should be visually clear as an infinite upmass source.

Source size for in-game sprite: 512 x 512 px.

Intended in-game display footprint: roughly 24-40 px wide, just larger than payload dots and endpoint markers.

Optional backstory/about-page version: 1024 x 1024 px or larger if used as an illustration outside the game canvas.

Composition:

- centered surface launcher object
- should be usable as an icon-like sprite or as a detail placed on the planet surface
- clear launch direction

Direction:

- cartoon-technical public works launcher
- clean and competently engineered
- can be a railgun, space cannon, or launch complex, as long as the direction of launch is obvious
- should not look improvised, damaged, or intentionally unsafe
- should be readable as a surface facility even at small scale

Resolved tone:

- clean civic mega-project
- technical enough to explain the role, not detailed enough to distract
