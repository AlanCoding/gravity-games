# Civic Prime Prompt Notes

Need a blue/green rocky planet that reads as a fictional habitable world, not Earth specifically.

Intended size: 2048 x 2048 px.

Composition:

- centered full planet disk viewed straight down along the north-pole axis
- transparent or simple dark background preferred if the generator supports it
- leave a little margin around the planet for clean cropping
- top-down polar map view with the icy north pole exactly in the center of the disk
- no side-view globe perspective, no tilted globe, no horizon, no pole near the top edge
- continents and oceans should form a radial polar projection around the central ice cap rather than showing recognizable Earth side-view geography

Direction:

- cartoon-technical blue/green rocky planet
- not Earth, but broadly habitable-looking
- arbitrary fictional landmasses, no recognizable Americas, Europe, Africa, Asia, Australia, or Earth map silhouette
- clean readable continents/oceans or terrain patches
- no surface launcher, no space gun, no buildings, no labels, no human-made infrastructure
- should resemble an explanatory diagram more than a realistic NASA render

Resolved tone:

- charming and diagrammatic, but still technical
- focus entirely on the fictional planet asset; the game will place launcher art separately
