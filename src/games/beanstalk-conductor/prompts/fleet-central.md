# Fleet Central Prompt Notes

Need a fixed-orbit logistics station / shipyard that reads as the customer destination for upmass and the source of
downmass.

Source size for in-game sprite: 512 x 512 px.

Intended in-game display footprint: roughly 32-48 px wide, just larger than payload dots and endpoint markers.

Optional backstory/about-page version: 1024 x 1024 px or larger if used as an illustration outside the game canvas.

Composition:

- centered station / shipyard object
- transparent or simple dark background preferred if the generator supports it
- strong silhouette for scaling down in the canvas

Direction:

- cartoon-technical station / shipyard, clean and operational
- intentionally vague station details are acceptable
- should read as large public or military logistics infrastructure
- avoid making it look broken, underfunded, rusty, or chaotic
- simple silhouette that can be recognized at small canvas scale

Resolved tone:

- practical shipyard plus naval bureaucracy
- clean and technical at first; dysfunction should come from gameplay, not the asset
