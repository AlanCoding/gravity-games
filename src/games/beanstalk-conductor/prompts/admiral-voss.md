# Admiral Voss Prompt Notes

Need cartoony portrait variants for Admiral Voss reacting to beanstalk operations.

Intended size: 1024 x 1024 px.

Composition:

- square portrait
- head and upper torso
- leave enough room around the head and shoulders for cropping in a dialog panel

Direction:

- both military and space-age
- clean futuristic uniform silhouette, but more decorated and military than minimalist starship pajamas
- decorated collar, shoulder marks, medals, or rank geometry are appropriate
- should still feel cartoony and readable, not realistic portraiture
- must not copy Star Trek uniforms, insignia, species, or protected franchise designs
- the military angle should be strong enough that anger, panic, and command frustration read clearly

Initial emotional beats:

- composed approval
- worried professionalism
- theatrical low-money panic
- collision / public-hearing catastrophe

Resolved tone:

- stern naval officer plus bureaucratic customer
- formal space military, clean and futuristic, with enough decoration to feel important
