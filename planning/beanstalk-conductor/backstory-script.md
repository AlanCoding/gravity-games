# Beanstalk Conductor Backstory Script

This is the first-pass script for the `Back story` menu choice. It should be implemented as a simple sequence of
illustrated panels with text. The final panel may later transition into a non-interactive demo of mass moving through
the clean theoretical ladder, but the first implementation does not require that.

## Panel 1: The Promise

Image: a clean cartoon-technical government podium or public announcement scene with `Civic Prime` and orbital
infrastructure diagrams behind it.

Text:

The `Deputy Undersecretary for Orbital Uplift` announced the `Strategic Space Elevator Initiative`.

`Civic Prime` would build a proper space elevator. The speeches were confident. The diagrams were vertical. The
borrowing was already underway.

## Panel 2: The Engineering Meeting

Image: engineers in a clean technical meeting room, pointing at diagrams that politely disagree with the official
announcement.

Text:

The engineers explained that the promised elevator was not physically practical on the promised schedule.

This was received as a communications problem.

## Panel 3: The Compromise

Image: a polished government slide rebranding the failed elevator as a beanstalk / tether railroad concept.

Text:

The `Office of Extraterrestrial Conveyance` introduced the `Emergency Vertical Access Compromise`.

It was not the space elevator anyone had promised. It was, however, close enough for the press release if nobody asked
too many follow-up questions.

## Panel 4: The Clean Beanstalk

Image: an explanatory cartoon-technical diagram of `Civic Prime`, a surface launcher, three barbell stages, and `Fleet
Central`.

Text:

The public version looked elegant.

Launch upmass from `Civic Prime`. Catch it with a barbell stage. Move it outward. Send downmass back. Keep the ladder
balanced. Build the fleet.

## Panel 5: Fleet Central

Image: `Fleet Central` as a clean space-age logistics station / shipyard accepting delivered cargo.

Text:

`Starward Logistics Command` needed mass delivered upward.

`Fleet Central` would accept useful cargo and provide downmass for the return flow. This sounded balanced, which is a
dangerous thing for an orbital system to sound.

## Panel 6: Admiral Voss

Image: `Admiral Voss`, decorated and space-age military, formally alarmed but still composed.

Text:

`Admiral Voss` was assigned to observe operations.

Space congress was angry. The money was borrowed. The compromise beanstalk had better work perfectly.

## Panel 7: Your Console

Image: the clean theoretical beanstalk system with highlighted timing controls and mass-transfer arrows.

Text:

You do not steer the payloads.

You choose when to commit each transfer. The solver computes the shot. The bill arrives afterward.

Optional later addition: show a non-interactive clean transfer demo before returning to the menu or starting the
tutorial.
